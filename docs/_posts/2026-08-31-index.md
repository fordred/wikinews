#### International relations

* [2026 Bishkek SCO summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Bishkek_SCO_summit "2026 Bishkek SCO summit")
  * The 26th [Shanghai Cooperation Organisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_Cooperation_Organisation "Shanghai Cooperation Organisation") (SCO) summit is held in [Bishkek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishkek "Bishkek"), [Kyrgyzstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrgyzstan "Kyrgyzstan"), which coincides with the country's [Independence Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_Day_%28Kyrgyzstan%29 "Independence Day (Kyrgyzstan)") and the opening ceremony of the 2026 [World Nomad Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Nomad_Games "World Nomad Games").   * A [South Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea "South Korea") [court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary_of_South_Korea "Judiciary of South Korea") sentences [Unification Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unification_Church "Unification Church") leader [Hak Ja Han](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hak_Ja_Han "Hak Ja Han") to two years in prison for [bribing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bribing "Bribing") officials, including former [first lady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Lady_of_South_Korea "First Lady of South Korea") [Kim Keon Hee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Keon_Hee "Kim Keon Hee"), in an effort to obtain [policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_South_Korea "Politics of South Korea") favors.
