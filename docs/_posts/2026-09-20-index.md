#### Armed conflicts and attacks

* [Russo-Ukrainian war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo-Ukrainian_war_%282022%E2%80%93present%29 "Russo-Ukrainian war (2022–present)")
  * [Attacks in Russia during the Russo-Ukrainian war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attacks_in_Russia_during_the_Russo-Ukrainian_war_%282022%E2%80%93present%29 "Attacks in Russia during the Russo-Ukrainian war (2022–present)")
    * [Ukrainian forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces_of_Ukraine "Armed Forces of Ukraine") launch a massive [drone attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drone_warfare "Drone warfare") on [Moscow Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Oblast "Moscow Oblast"), [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia "Russia"), with more than 1,600 long-range drones launched from [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine "Ukraine"). At least two people are killed with hundreds more evacuated while [Moscow Refinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Refinery "Moscow Refinery") is on fire following several direct hits. [(Al Jazeera)](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/20/mass-ukrainian-drone-attack-on-moscow-kills-two-russia-says)
    * [Ukrainian president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Ukraine "President of Ukraine") [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy "Volodymyr Zelenskyy") confirms he ordered the attack on [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow "Moscow") in response to [Russian forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Armed_Forces "Russian Armed Forces") targeting civilians in Ukrainian cities, saying that [FP-5 Flamingo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FP-5_Flamingo "FP-5 Flamingo") [cruise missiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruise_missile "Cruise missile") and [ballistic missile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballistic_missile "Ballistic missile") were also used in the attack. [(L'Unione Sarda)](https://www.unionesarda.it/en/world/massive-attack-on-moscow-chaos-at-the-airport-zelensky-quot-cruise-and-ballistic-missiles-were-also-usedquot-tcsco6db)
