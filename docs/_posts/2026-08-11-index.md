#### International relations

* [Syria and weapons of mass destruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syria_and_weapons_of_mass_destruction "Syria and weapons of mass destruction")
  * The [Syrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syria "Syria") [government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Syria "Government of Syria") confirms their cooperation with the [International Atomic Energy Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Atomic_Energy_Agency "International Atomic Energy Agency") to purge [nuclear materials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_material "Nuclear material") at a hidden facility following a report by *[Axios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axios_%28website%29 "Axios (website)")* on such plan after an agreement between [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel "Israel"), Syria, and the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States "United States"). Conversely, Israel threatens to bombard the site again if the materials remain, and to prevent access to the site. [(AA)](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/middle-east/iaea-to-remove-nuclear-material-from-clandestine-syrian-site-report/4023605) [(*Axios*)](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/10/syria-nuclear-israel-turkey-iaea) [(*The Jerusalem Post*)](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/article-905121)

#### Law and crime

#### Politics and elections

* [2026 Hungarian presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Hungarian_presidential_election "2026 Hungarian presidential election")
  * Former [Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curia_of_Hungary "Curia of Hungary") president [András Baka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A1s_Baka "András Baka") is elected as [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary "Hungary") [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Hungary "President of Hungary") through a [supermajority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supermajority "Supermajority") and is scheduled to take office on 19 August. He ran [uncontested](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uncontested_election "Uncontested election") as the [Tisza Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tisza_Party "Tisza Party")'s candidate, as neither [Fidesz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fidesz "Fidesz") nor its partner [KNDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Democratic_People%27s_Party_%28Hungary%29 "Christian Democratic People's Party (Hungary)") nominated a candidate.
