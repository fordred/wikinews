#### International relations

* [Syria and weapons of mass destruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syria_and_weapons_of_mass_destruction "Syria and weapons of mass destruction")
  * The [government of Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Syria "Government of Syria") confirms their cooperation with the [International Atomic Energy Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Atomic_Energy_Agency "International Atomic Energy Agency") to purge nuclear materials at a hidden facility in [Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syria "Syria"), following a report by [Axios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axios_%28website%29 "Axios (website)") on such plan after an agreement between [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel "Israel"), Syria and the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States "United States"). Conversely, Israel threatens to bombard the site again if the materials remain, and to prevent access to the site. [(Anadolu Agency)](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/middle-east/iaea-to-remove-nuclear-material-from-clandestine-syrian-site-report/4023605) [(*The Jerusalen Post*)](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/article-905121) [(Axios)](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/10/syria-nuclear-israel-turkey-iaea)

#### Law and crime

* [Capital punishment in Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_punishment_in_Syria "Capital punishment in Syria")
  * The Criminal Court in Damascus sentenced deposed President [Bashar al-Assad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bashar_al-Assad "Bashar al-Assad") and his brother [Maher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maher_al-Assad "Maher al-Assad") to [death in absentia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_in_absentia "Death in absentia"), and [Atef Najib](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atef_Najib "Atef Najib")—former Political Security director in [Daraa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daraa_Governorate "Daraa Governorate")—in person. The rulings cite [premeditated murder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premeditated_murder "Premeditated murder") and [crimes against humanity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crimes_against_humanity "Crimes against humanity"). Arrest warrants were simultaneously issued for all fugitives. [(The National)](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/2026/08/11/damascus-court-sentences-bashar-al-assad-and-others-to-death/)
