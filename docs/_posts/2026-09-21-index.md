The [Joint Chiefs of Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Chiefs_of_Staff_%28South_Korea%29 "Joint Chiefs of Staff (South Korea)") says the cause remains unknown. #### Health and environment

* [New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand "New Zealand")'s [critically endangered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critically_endangered "Critically endangered") [black robin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_robin "Black robin") wins [Forest & Bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_%26_Bird "Forest & Bird")'s [Bird of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bird_of_the_Year "Bird of the Year") competition with more than 26,000 votes out of about 105,000 votes.
