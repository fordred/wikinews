#### Science and technology

* [Solar eclipse of August 12, 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_eclipse_of_August_12%2C_2026 "Solar eclipse of August 12, 2026")
  * A total [solar eclipse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_eclipse "Solar eclipse") occurs in [Greenland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland "Greenland"), [Iceland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iceland "Iceland") and [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain "Spain"), with partial eclipses visible over much of northern [North America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_America "North America"), [Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europe "Europe") (except most of [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine "Ukraine"), [Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece "Greece"), [Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgaria "Bulgaria") and [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia "Russia")), northwestern [Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Africa "Africa") and northern [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia "Asia") [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia "Russia"). It is the first total eclipse observable in mainland Europe since the [solar eclipse of August 11, 1999](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_eclipse_of_August_11%2C_1999 "Solar eclipse of August 11, 1999").
