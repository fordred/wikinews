#### Law and crime

* The [Pacific Islands Forum Fisheries Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Islands_Forum_Fisheries_Agency "Pacific Islands Forum Fisheries Agency") reports that authorities in [Kiribati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiribati "Kiribati"), [Palau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palau "Palau"), and [Papua New Guinea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papua_New_Guinea "Papua New Guinea") have detained four [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fishing_industry_in_China "Fishing industry in China") and [Indonesian fishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquaculture_in_Indonesia "Aquaculture in Indonesia") vessels for alleged [illegal fishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illegal_fishing "Illegal fishing") or licence violations during a regional enforcement operation across the [Pacific Ocean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Ocean "Pacific Ocean"). [(AFP via *Daily Tribune*)](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/08/21/pacific-nations-detain-chinese-indonesian-boats-over-illegal-fishing)
