#### Armed conflicts and attacks

* In [Muksudpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muksudpur_Upazila "Muksudpur Upazila"), [Gopalganj District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gopalganj_District%2C_Bangladesh "Gopalganj District, Bangladesh"), [Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh"), members of the banned [Awami League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awami_League "Awami League") [loot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Looting "Looting") and [set fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arson "Arson") to the ancestral home of [BNP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Nationalist_Party "Bangladesh Nationalist Party") [Member of Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jatiya_Sangsad "Jatiya Sangsad") [Md. Selimuzzaman Mollah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Md._Selimuzzaman_Mollah "Md. Selimuzzaman Mollah") of [Gopalganj-1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gopalganj-1 "Gopalganj-1"), several supporters houses, an unoccupied [police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Police "Bangladesh Police") station along with at least ten parked vehicles, and a [Grameen Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grameen_Bank "Grameen Bank") branch, while attackers in [Kashiani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashiani_Upazila "Kashiani Upazila") [vandalise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vandalism "Vandalism") residences and businesses linked to BNP and [Jamaat-e-Islami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Jamaat-e-Islami "Bangladesh Jamaat-e-Islami") leaders and throw bricks at [Border Guard Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_Guard_Bangladesh "Border Guard Bangladesh") and [Special Response Battalion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Response_Battalion "Special Response Battalion") vehicles. Separately, armed attackers loot and set fire to the ancestral home and three trucks of jailed former [Feni-3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feni-3 "Feni-3") MP [Rahim Ullah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rahim_Ullah "Rahim Ullah") in [Sonagazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonagazi_Upazila "Sonagazi Upazila"), [Feni District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feni_District "Feni District"), while unidentified attackers set fire to the residence of former [Minister of State for Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_%28Bangladesh%29 "Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Bangladesh)") [Shahriar Alam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahriar_Alam "Shahriar Alam") in [Bagha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagha_Upazila "Bagha Upazila"), [Rajshahi District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajshahi_District "Rajshahi District"), before local residents extinguish the blaze.
