#### Armed conflicts and attacks

* [Bulgaria–Russia relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgaria%E2%80%93Russia_relations "Bulgaria–Russia relations")
  * Two [merchant ships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merchant_ship "Merchant ship") are hit by unknown [drones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drone_warfare "Drone warfare") off [Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgaria "Bulgaria")'s [Black Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sea "Black Sea") coast, with one [sinking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shipwrecking "Shipwrecking"). [Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Bulgaria "Prime Minister of Bulgaria") [Rumen Radev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rumen_Radev "Rumen Radev") has blamed [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia "Russia"), calling its [ambassador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ambassadors_of_Russia_to_Bulgaria "List of ambassadors of Russia to Bulgaria"), without providing evidence. It is the second such attack in six days. [(Reuters)](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/drone-sinks-ship-off-bulgaria-coast-prime-minister-says-2026-10-13/)
