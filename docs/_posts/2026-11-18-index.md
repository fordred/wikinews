#### Business and economy

* [2026 Bangladesh garment workers protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Bangladesh_garment_workers_protests "2026 Bangladesh garment workers protests")
  * In [Gazipur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gazipur "Gazipur"), [Savar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savar_Upazila "Savar Upazila"), and [Ashulia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashulia "Ashulia"), [Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh"), thousands of [garment workers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textile_industry_in_Bangladesh "Textile industry in Bangladesh") demanding a monthly [minimum wage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_wage "Minimum wage") increase from [৳](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladeshi_taka "Bangladeshi taka")12,500 ([US$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US%24 "US$")100) to ৳30,000 ($240) block the [Dhaka–Mymensingh Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N3_%28Bangladesh%29 "N3 (Bangladesh)") and set fire to a [police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Police "Bangladesh Police") vehicle, causing police to fire [tear gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tear_gas "Tear gas") and [stun grenades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stun_grenade "Stun grenade") in Gazipur and Ashulia as 55 [factories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factory "Factory") close across the three areas, while police report around 300 [arrests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrest "Arrest") across 24 cases filed in Savar and Ashulia during the protests. [(*The Daily Star*)](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/news/police-vehicle-torched-garment-workers-block-dhaka-mymensingh-highway-4326951) [(*The Daily Star*)](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/news/workers-unrest-continues-savar-ashulia-25-factories-shut-4327056)
  * In [Kafrul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kafrul_Thana "Kafrul Thana"), [Dhaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhaka "Dhaka"), police fire around ten stun grenades against demonstrating garment workers following clashes with [Bangladesh Nationalist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Nationalist_Party "Bangladesh Nationalist Party") activists who allegedly [harassed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harassment "Harassment") female factory employees, causing workers to stage demonstrations and [halt production](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strike_action "Strike action") at several nearby [garment factories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clothing_industry "Clothing industry"). [(*The Business Standard*)](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/police-garment-workers-clash-kafrul-over-alleged-harassment-female-workers-1606131)
