#### Politics and elections

* [2026 Czech Senate election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Czech_Senate_election "2026 Czech Senate election")
  * In [Czechia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechia "Czechia"), [opposition parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_political_parties_in_the_Czech_Republic "List of political parties in the Czech Republic") win 22 of the contested 27 seats in the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Senate "Czech Senate"). The lead party of the [government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_cabinet_of_Andrej_Babi%C5%A1 "Third cabinet of Andrej Babiš") of [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_the_Czech_Republic "Prime Minister of the Czech Republic") [Andrej Babiš](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrej_Babi%C5%A1 "Andrej Babiš"), [ANO 2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ANO_2011 "ANO 2011") comes in third place. The election is the first time in the country's history where governing parties did not win a single seat in a senatorial election. [(AP)](https://apnews.com/article/czech-republic-senate-election-babis-d206f3edbc7ea0f73b0dbd07b339820b)
