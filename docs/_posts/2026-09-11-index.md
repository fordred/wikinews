  * A research study at the [Icahn School of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Icahn_School_of_Medicine_at_Mount_Sinai "Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai") at [Mount Sinai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Sinai_Health_System "Mount Sinai Health System") reports that, in the [aftermath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aftermath_of_the_September_11_attacks "Aftermath of the September 11 attacks") of the [September 11 attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/September_11_attacks "September 11 attacks"), [World Trade Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Trade_Center_%281973%E2%80%932001%29 "World Trade Center (1973–2001)") responders living in greener neighborhoods had lower risks of frailty, [PTSD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-traumatic_stress_disorder "Post-traumatic stress disorder"), and [depression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_depressive_disorder "Major depressive disorder"). Greater exposure to heat and air pollution was associated with higher risks of these conditions. [(ABC News)](https://abcnews.com/Health/residential-air-pollution-linked-higher-depression-scores-911/story?id=136361763) [(Mount Sinai)](https://www.mountsinai.org/about/newsroom/2026/25-years-after-9-11-study-finds-neighborhood-environment-may-influence-frailty-ptsd-and-depression-risk-among-world-trade-center-responders)
