#### Health and environment

* [Endangerment of orangutans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endangerment_of_orangutans "Endangerment of orangutans"), [India–Indonesia relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India%E2%80%93Indonesia_relations "India–Indonesia relations")
  * [Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesia "Indonesia") works with [Indian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India "India") authorities to repatriate five baby [orangutans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orangutan "Orangutan") found in an [Odisha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odisha "Odisha") [forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forests_in_Odisha "Forests in Odisha") after suspected [trafficking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildlife_smuggling "Wildlife smuggling"). [Odisha Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odisha_Police "Odisha Police") and India's [Wildlife Crime Control Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildlife_Crime_Control_Bureau "Wildlife Crime Control Bureau") examine how the apes, which are native to [Borneo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borneo "Borneo") and [Sumatra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumatra "Sumatra"), reached India. [(AFP via France 24)](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20260911-indonesia-to-bring-back-baby-orangutans-allegedly-trafficked-to-india)

#### Law and crime

* A [Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong "Hong Kong") [court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary_of_Hong_Kong "Judiciary of Hong Kong") sentences [pro-democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pro-democracy_camp_%28Hong_Kong%29 "Pro-democracy camp (Hong Kong)") activists [Lee Cheuk-yan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Cheuk-yan "Lee Cheuk-yan") and [Chow Hang-tung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chow_Hang-tung "Chow Hang-tung") to seven years in [prison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prisons_in_Hong_Kong "Prisons in Hong Kong") each for [inciting subversion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inciting_subversion_of_state_power "Inciting subversion of state power") under the [national security law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Hong_Kong_national_security_law "2020 Hong Kong national security law") for leading the [Hong Kong Alliance in Support of Patriotic Democratic Movements of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_Alliance_in_Support_of_Patriotic_Democratic_Movements_of_China "Hong Kong Alliance in Support of Patriotic Democratic Movements of China").
