#### Law and crime

* [War crimes in the Myanmar civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_crimes_in_the_Myanmar_civil_war_%282021%E2%80%93present%29 "War crimes in the Myanmar civil war (2021–present)")
  * [Human Rights Watch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Rights_Watch "Human Rights Watch") reports that [women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_in_Myanmar "Women in Myanmar") held as [political prisoners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_prisoner "Political prisoner") in [Myanmar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanmar "Myanmar") face [torture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torture_in_Myanmar "Torture in Myanmar"), [sexual violence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wartime_sexual_violence "Wartime sexual violence"), and inadequate [medical care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_in_Myanmar "Health in Myanmar"). The group says more than 6,600 women and 640 children are among over 30,000 political prisoners detained since 2021. [(Reuters)](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/myanmar-female-prisoners-face-torture-neglect-rights-group-says-2026-09-23/)

#### Politics and elections

* [Second impeachment of Sara Duterte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_impeachment_of_Sara_Duterte "Second impeachment of Sara Duterte")
