#### Business and economy

* [2026 Bangladesh garment workers protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Bangladesh_garment_workers_protests "2026 Bangladesh garment workers protests")
  * Amid [minimum wage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_wage "Minimum wage") protests by [garment workers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clothing_industry "Clothing industry"), 13 [garment factories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textile_industry_in_Bangladesh "Textile industry in Bangladesh") in [Gazipur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gazipur_District "Gazipur District") are shut indefinitely under the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 and over 50 remain closed in [Savar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savar_Upazila "Savar Upazila") and [Ashulia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashulia "Ashulia"), while 48 [platoons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platoon "Platoon") of the [Border Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_Guard_Bangladesh "Border Guard Bangladesh"), [Ansar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Ansar "Bangladesh Ansar"), and [Special Response Battalion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Response_Battalion "Special Response Battalion") deploy across industrial zones in [Dhaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhaka_District "Dhaka District"), Gazipur, and [Narayanganj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narayanganj_District "Narayanganj District"). [(*The Business Standard*)](https://www.tbsnews.net/economy/rmg/13-factories-shut-indefinitely-gazipur-amid-labour-unrest-holiday-declared-five-1602791)
