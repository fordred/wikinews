#### International relations

* [Australia–Fiji relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia%E2%80%93Fiji_relations "Australia–Fiji relations")
  * [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia "Australia") agrees to provide [Fiji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiji "Fiji") with solar-, wind- and [wave-powered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave-powered "Wave-powered") [Bluebottle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bluebottle_%28uncrewed_surface_vessels%29 "Bluebottle (uncrewed surface vessels)") [maritime drones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maritime_drone "Maritime drone") and training for [surveillance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surveillance "Surveillance") and efforts against [drug trafficking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_trafficking "Drug trafficking") and other [transnational crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transnational_crime "Transnational crime"), with deployment expected in 2027.
