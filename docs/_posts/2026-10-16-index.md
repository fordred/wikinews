#### Health and environment

* [Deforestation of the Amazon rainforest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deforestation_of_the_Amazon_rainforest "Deforestation of the Amazon rainforest")
  * [Brazilian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil "Brazil") [environment minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Environment_and_Climate_Change_%28Brazil%29 "Ministry of Environment and Climate Change (Brazil)") [João Paulo Capobianco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jo%C3%A3o_Paulo_Capobianco?action=edit&redlink=1 "João Paulo Capobianco (page does not exist)") [[pt](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jo%C3%A3o%20Paulo%20Capobianco "pt:João Paulo Capobianco")] reports that [deforestation in Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deforestation_in_Brazil "Deforestation in Brazil")'s [Amazon rainforest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_rainforest "Amazon rainforest") fell to 5,796 km2 (2,238 sq mi) in the year through July 2026, the lowest level since 1988 except for 2012. [(AFP via France 24)](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20261016-deforestation-in-brazil-amazon-hits-nearly-four-decade-low)
