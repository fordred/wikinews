#### International relations

* [Australia–Fiji relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia%E2%80%93Fiji_relations "Australia–Fiji relations")
  * [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia "Australia") agrees to provide [Fiji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiji "Fiji") with solar-, wind- and [wave-powered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave-powered "Wave-powered") [Bluebottle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bluebottle_%28uncrewed_surface_vessels%29 "Bluebottle (uncrewed surface vessels)") [maritime drones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maritime_drone "Maritime drone") and training for [surveillance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surveillance "Surveillance") and efforts against [drug trafficking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_trafficking "Drug trafficking") and other [transnational crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transnational_crime "Transnational crime"), with deployment expected in 2027. [(AFP via RFI)](https://www.rfi.fr/en/international-news/20261016-fiji-to-deploy-solar-powered-australian-military-drones-at-sea)
* [Hungary–Ukraine relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary%E2%80%93Ukraine_relations "Hungary–Ukraine relations")
  * [Hungarian prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Hungary "Prime Minister of Hungary") [Péter Magyar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C3%A9ter_Magyar "Péter Magyar") and [Ukrainian president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Ukraine "President of Ukraine") [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy "Volodymyr Zelenskyy") agree to improve bilateral relations during a meeting in [Brussels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brussels "Brussels"), [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium "Belgium"), with [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary "Hungary") lifting objections to further [Ukrainian European Union accession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accession_of_Ukraine_to_the_European_Union "Accession of Ukraine to the European Union") talks following progress on [ethnic Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarians_in_Ukraine "Hungarians in Ukraine") [minority rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minority_rights "Minority rights") in [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine "Ukraine"). [(Reuters)](https://www.reuters.com/world/hungary-ukraine-agree-reset-ties-magyar-says-2026-10-16/)
