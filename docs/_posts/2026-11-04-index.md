#### Business and economy

* The [Bangladeshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh") [garment industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textile_industry_in_Bangladesh "Textile industry in Bangladesh") [wage board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worker_standards_board "Worker standards board") agrees to increase [minimum wages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_wage "Minimum wage") following five days of [worker protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strike_action "Strike action") and clashes with [police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Police "Bangladesh Police"). Workers demand [৳](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladeshi_taka "Bangladeshi taka")30,000 ([US$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US%24 "US$")240) monthly, up from ৳12,500 ($100), while the board has not specified the increase. [(AFP)](https://www.afp.com/en/bangladesh-increase-garment-sector-pay-after-protests)
