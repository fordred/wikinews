#### Business and economy

* The [Bangladeshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh") [garment industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textile_industry_in_Bangladesh "Textile industry in Bangladesh") [wage board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worker_standards_board "Worker standards board") agrees to increase [minimum wages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_wage "Minimum wage") following five days of [worker protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strike_action "Strike action") and clashes with [police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Police "Bangladesh Police"). Workers demand [৳](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladeshi_taka "Bangladeshi taka")30,000 ([US$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US%24 "US$")240) monthly, up from ৳12,500 (US$100), while the board has not specified the increase. [(AFP)](https://www.afp.com/en/bangladesh-increase-garment-sector-pay-after-protests)

#### Health and environment

* [2026 Southeast Asian haze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Southeast_Asian_haze "2026 Southeast Asian haze")
  * The [Singaporean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore "Singapore") [parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Singapore "Parliament of Singapore") amends the [Transboundary Haze Pollution Act of 2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transboundary_Haze_Pollution_Act_of_2014 "Transboundary Haze Pollution Act of 2014"), introducing [arrest powers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement_in_Singapore "Law enforcement in Singapore"), permitting [trials *in absentia*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trial_in_absentia "Trial in absentia"), and increasing maximum fines tenfold to [S$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%24 "S$")20 million (US$15 million) following widespread [air pollution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_issues_in_Singapore "Environmental issues in Singapore") from [Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesia "Indonesia") [wildfires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Indonesia_wildfires "2026 Indonesia wildfires"). [(AFP)](https://www.afp.com/en/singapore-toughens-haze-law-arrest-powers-bigger-fines)
* The [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom "United Kingdom")'s [Office for National Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_for_National_Statistics "Office for National Statistics") reports that deaths linked to [cocaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocaine "Cocaine") use have overtaken deaths from [heroin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroin "Heroin") in [England and Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_and_Wales "England and Wales") for the first time on record. The increase in deaths from cocaine use is blamed on increased purity of the drug and its widespread availability across the UK in recent years. [(BBC News)](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmdxw051np9jo)
