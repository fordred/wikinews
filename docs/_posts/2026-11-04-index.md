#### Business and economy

* The [Bangladeshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh") [garment industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textile_industry_in_Bangladesh "Textile industry in Bangladesh") [wage board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worker_standards_board "Worker standards board") agrees to increase [minimum wages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_wage "Minimum wage") following five days of [worker protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strike_action "Strike action") and clashes with [police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Police "Bangladesh Police"). Workers demand [৳](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladeshi_taka "Bangladeshi taka")30,000 ([US$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US%24 "US$")240) monthly, up from ৳12,500 (US$100), while the board has not specified the increase. [(AFP)](https://www.afp.com/en/bangladesh-increase-garment-sector-pay-after-protests)

#### Disasters and accidents

* A motorized [cargo boat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cargo_boat "Cargo boat") carrying a load of [copra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copra "Copra") capsizes off [Jomalig, Quezon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jomalig%2C_Quezon "Jomalig, Quezon"), [Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippines "Philippines"). A [search and rescue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Search_and_rescue "Search and rescue") operation is later launched for seven missing individuals aboard. [(Inquirer.net)](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/2336548/7-men-missing-after-cargo-boat-capsizes-off-jomalig-quezon/)

#### Law and crime

* [Philippine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippines "Philippines") authorities seize a 200-kilogram (440 lb) shipment of [methamphetamine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methamphetamine "Methamphetamine"), whose origin is traced to [Laos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laos "Laos"), at the [Manila International Container Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Manila "Port of Manila"). The shipment had arrived six days after local police in Laos [found the similar contraband, supposed to be brought to the Philippines,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Laos_private_jet_drug_smuggling_case "2026 Laos private jet drug smuggling case") on October 1. #### Politics and elections

* [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary "Hungary") makes [Communist-era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_People%27s_Republic "Hungarian People's Republic") [secret police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secret_police "Secret police") records publicly accessible through a [database](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Database "Database") containing approximately 60,000 names linked to former [state security services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Internal_Affairs_III "Ministry of Internal Affairs III"). [Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Hungary "Prime Minister of Hungary") [Péter Magyar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C3%A9ter_Magyar "Péter Magyar") announces that additional documents will become available in February 2027. * The [Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Gehrels_Swift_Observatory "Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory"), a [space telescope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_telescope "Space telescope") for studying [gamma-ray bursts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamma-ray_burst "Gamma-ray burst"), [re-enters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_entry#Uncontrolled_and_unprotected_entries "Atmospheric entry") Earth's atmosphere over [Sumatra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumatra "Sumatra"), ending its mission of nearly 22 years.
