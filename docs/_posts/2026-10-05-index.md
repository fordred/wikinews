#### Politics and elections

* [2025–2026 Kosovo political deadlock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%932026_Kosovo_political_deadlock "2025–2026 Kosovo political deadlock")
  * In [Kosovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kosovo "Kosovo"), the [acting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acting_%28law%29 "Acting (law)") [presidency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Kosovo "President of Kosovo") of [Albulena Haxhiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albulena_Haxhiu "Albulena Haxhiu") became *[de facto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_facto "De facto")* due to her having continuously served in the position for more than six months since assuming office on 4 April, violating the third paragraph of Article 90 in the [constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Kosovo "Constitution of Kosovo"). The [Kuvendi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assembly_of_Kosovo "Assembly of Kosovo"), which is set to meet for amendments to the [Kosovo Specialist Chambers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kosovo_Specialist_Chambers "Kosovo Specialist Chambers"), has two days left until the [presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Kosovan_presidential_election "2026 Kosovan presidential election") deadline by the [Constitutional Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Court_of_Kosovo "Constitutional Court of Kosovo") expires.
