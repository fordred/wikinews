#### Politics and elections

  * [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia "Russia") begins holding an election for 450 seats of the [State Duma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Duma "State Duma") over a period of three days amid [Ukrainian attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attacks_in_Russia_during_the_Russo-Ukrainian_war_%282022%E2%80%93present%29 "Attacks in Russia during the Russo-Ukrainian war (2022–present)") upon oil infrastructure.
