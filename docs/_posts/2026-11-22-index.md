#### Business and economy

* [2026 Bangladesh garment workers protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Bangladesh_garment_workers_protests "2026 Bangladesh garment workers protests")
  * Several [garment factories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textile_industry_in_Bangladesh "Textile industry in Bangladesh") in [Gazipur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gazipur "Gazipur"), [Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh"), declare holidays amid ongoing [minimum wage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_wage "Minimum wage") protests, as clashes erupt between [workers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Working_class "Working class") and [police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Police "Bangladesh Police") along the [Dhaka–Mymensingh Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N3_%28Bangladesh%29 "N3 (Bangladesh)"), with workers throwing bricks and police firing [tear gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tear_gas "Tear gas") and [stun grenades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stun_grenade "Stun grenade") while [Special Response Battalion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Response_Battalion "Special Response Battalion"), [Ansar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Ansar "Bangladesh Ansar"), [BGB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_Guard_Bangladesh "Border Guard Bangladesh"), and [Industrial Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_Police_%28Bangladesh%29 "Industrial Police (Bangladesh)") personnel deploy to the area. [(*The Business Standard*)](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/garment-workers-stage-fresh-protest-gazipur-attempt-block-dhaka-mymensingh-highway)
  * [Border Guard Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_Guard_Bangladesh "Border Guard Bangladesh") deploys 95 [platoons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platoon "Platoon") across seven districts, including two [battalions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") in [Gopalganj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gopalganj_District%2C_Bangladesh "Gopalganj District, Bangladesh"), stepping up [surveillance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surveillance "Surveillance") at key installations and industrial zones using [drones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unmanned_aerial_vehicle "Unmanned aerial vehicle") and [police body cameras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_body_camera "Police body camera"). [(*The Daily Star*)](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/crime-justice/news/bgb-deploys-95-platoons-across-seven-districts-maintain-law-and-order-4330136)
