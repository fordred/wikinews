#### Health and environment

* [Environmental policy of the second Trump administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_policy_of_the_second_Trump_administration "Environmental policy of the second Trump administration")
  * The [United States Fish and Wildlife Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Fish_and_Wildlife_Service "United States Fish and Wildlife Service") narrows its enforcement of the [Endangered Species Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endangered_Species_Act_of_1973 "Endangered Species Act of 1973") by excluding unintentional [wildlife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fauna_of_the_United_States "Fauna of the United States") deaths from its definition of prohibited [take](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Take_%28hunting%29 "Take (hunting)"), including foreseeable deaths linked to activities such as [commercial fishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fishing_industry_in_the_United_States "Fishing industry in the United States") and [logging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_lumber_industry_in_the_United_States "History of the lumber industry in the United States"). [(AFP via France 24)](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20260917-trump-admin-overhauls-key-protection-for-endangered-species)
* Biologists publish a study in the journal *[Current Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Biology "Current Biology")* describing a new species of [wild cat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildcat "Wildcat") belonging to the genus *[Leopardus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leopardus "Leopardus")*, commonly called the "tilcayo", in the [Yungas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolivian_Yungas "Bolivian Yungas") [cloud forests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud_forest "Cloud forest") of [Bolivia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolivia "Bolivia"). This discovery marks the first new cat species in over 100 years. [(*Cell*)](https://www.doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2026.08.059) [(Reuters)](https://www.reuters.com/business/environment/new-cat-species-identified-bolivia-first-over-century-2026-09-17/)
