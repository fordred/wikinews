#### Disasters and accidents

* [Sinking of the Virgo Transport 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinking_of_the_Virgo_Transport_8?action=edit&redlink=1 "Sinking of the Virgo Transport 8 (page does not exist)")
