#### Politics and elections

* [2026 Swedish general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Swedish_general_election "2026 Swedish general election")
  * [Swedes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedes "Swedes") vote to elect all 349 seats of the [Riksdag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riksdag "Riksdag"). Incumbent [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Sweden "Prime Minister of Sweden") [Ulf Kristersson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulf_Kristersson "Ulf Kristersson"), who seeks to lead the [Moderates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moderate_Party "Moderate Party") to a second consecutive government while including the [right-wing populist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right-wing_populist "Right-wing populist") [Democrats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden_Democrats "Sweden Democrats") in his cabinet, is being challenged by the former prime minister and [SAP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Social_Democratic_Party "Swedish Social Democratic Party") leader [Magdalena Andersson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magdalena_Andersson "Magdalena Andersson").
