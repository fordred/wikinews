#### Politics and elections

* [2026 Swedish general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Swedish_general_election "2026 Swedish general election")
  * [Swedes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedes "Swedes") vote to elect all 349 seats of the [Riksdag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riksdag "Riksdag"). Incumbent [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Sweden "Prime Minister of Sweden") [Ulf Kristersson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulf_Kristersson "Ulf Kristersson"), who seeks to lead the [Moderates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moderate_Party "Moderate Party") to a second consecutive government while including the [right-wing populist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right-wing_populist "Right-wing populist") [Sweden Democrats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden_Democrats "Sweden Democrats") in his cabinet, is being challenged by the former prime minister and [SAP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Social_Democratic_Party "Swedish Social Democratic Party") leader [Magdalena Andersson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magdalena_Andersson "Magdalena Andersson"). Preliminary results indicate that the [Red-Greens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red-Greens_%28Sweden%29 "Red-Greens (Sweden)") and the [Centre Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_Party_%28Sweden%29 "Centre Party (Sweden)") are projected to have a combined total of 175 seats, while the four [Tidö parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tid%C3%B6_Agreement "Tidö Agreement") are projected to have a total of 174 seats. [(AP)](https://apnews.com/article/sweden-election-parliament-kristersson-andersson-6671108eecef137f7bb9a9f545983c23) [(DW)](https://www.dw.com/en/sweden-election-far-right-eyes-power-for-1st-time/a-79248599)
* [Kosovo political deadlock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%932026_Kosovo_political_deadlock "2025–2026 Kosovo political deadlock")
  * The [Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assembly_of_Kosovo "Assembly of Kosovo") of [Kosovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kosovo "Kosovo") is declared constituted by [acting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acting_%28law%29 "Acting (law)") [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Kosovo "President of Kosovo") and [speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_Assembly_of_Kosovo "Speaker of the Assembly of Kosovo") [Albulena Haxhiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albulena_Haxhiu "Albulena Haxhiu"), but without the deputy speaker from the [PDK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_of_Kosovo "Democratic Party of Kosovo"). The Assembly then accepts the [fourth cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_Cabinet_of_Albin_Kurti "Fourth Cabinet of Albin Kurti") of incumbent [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Kosovo "Prime Minister of Kosovo") [Albin Kurti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albin_Kurti "Albin Kurti"). The PDK and the [AAK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliance_for_the_Future_of_Kosovo "Alliance for the Future of Kosovo") both challenge the legitimacy of Kurti's government at the [Constitutional Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Court_of_Kosovo "Constitutional Court of Kosovo"). [(AP)](https://apnews.com/article/kosovo-government-kurti-crisis-5c3727cfbcf04a3cc5734972fa6ea6c0) [(Reuters)](https://www.reuters.com/world/kosovo-parliament-re-elects-albin-kurti-prime-minister-2026-09-13/)
