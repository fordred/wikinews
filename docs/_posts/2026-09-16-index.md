* [Discoveries of exoplanets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discoveries_of_exoplanets "Discoveries of exoplanets")
  * Astronomers confirm the existence of [Elias 2-24 b](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elias_2-24_b?action=edit&redlink=1 "Elias 2-24 b (page does not exist)"), the [youngest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_exoplanet_extremes#Planetary_characteristics "List of exoplanet extremes") known [exoplanet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exoplanet "Exoplanet"), which is less than one million years old and located approximately 450 light-years from [Earth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth "Earth"). The discovery was observed through the [W.M. Keck Observatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W.M._Keck_Observatory "W.M. Keck Observatory") in [Mauna Kea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauna_Kea "Mauna Kea"), [Hawaii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaii "Hawaii"), U.S., using a [coronagraph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronagraph "Coronagraph"). [(Space.com)](https://www.space.com/astronomy/exoplanets/scientists-find-youngest-exoplanet-on-record-a-newborn-named-elias)
