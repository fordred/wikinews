#### Politics and elections

* [2026 Bangsamoro Parliament election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Bangsamoro_Parliament_election "2026 Bangsamoro Parliament election")
  * The [Philippine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippines "Philippines") [Commission on Elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission_on_Elections_%28Philippines%29 "Commission on Elections (Philippines)") proclaims all 78 elected members of the [Bangsamoro Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangsamoro_Parliament "Bangsamoro Parliament"), with four parties given all seats for party representation. The [Bangsamoro Federalist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangsamoro_Federalist_Party "Bangsamoro Federalist Party"), earning most of the seats, fails to secure a majority, leading to a [hung parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hung_parliament "Hung parliament"). Meanwhile, six sectoral seats are given to the [United Bangsamoro Justice Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Bangsamoro_Justice_Party "United Bangsamoro Justice Party"). [(Al Jazeera)](https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/news/2026/9/15/election-in-philippines-bangsamoro-region-ends-in-hung-parliament) [(*Manila Bulletin*)](https://mb.com.ph/2026/09/16/comelec-proclaims-winning-parties-bets-in-barmm-first-parliamentary-polls)
