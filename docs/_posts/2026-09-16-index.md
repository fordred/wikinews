#### Politics and elections

* [2026 Bangsamoro Parliament election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Bangsamoro_Parliament_election "2026 Bangsamoro Parliament election")
  * The [Philippine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippines "Philippines") [Commission on Elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission_on_Elections_%28Philippines%29 "Commission on Elections (Philippines)") proclaims all 78 elected members of the [Bangsamoro Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangsamoro_Parliament "Bangsamoro Parliament"), with four parties given all seats for party representation.
