    * The [Abu Dhabi National Oil Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Dhabi_National_Oil_Company "Abu Dhabi National Oil Company") says that one of their vessels were attacked by a missile while transiting through the [Strait of Hormuz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Hormuz "Strait of Hormuz") during the early hours.
