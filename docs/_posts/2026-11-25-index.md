[(*The Business Standard*)](https://www.tbsnews.net/economy/rmg/police-fire-tear-gas-sound-grenades-garment-workers-block-dhaka-tangail-highway-1613231) [(*The Daily Star*)](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/news/87-garment-factories-shut-amid-protests-gazipur-savar-ashulia-4332861)
