#### Business and economy

* [2026 Bangladesh garment workers protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Bangladesh_garment_workers_protests "2026 Bangladesh garment workers protests")
  * [Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh")'s [Minimum Wage Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_Wage_Board "Minimum Wage Board") raises the monthly [minimum wage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_wage "Minimum wage") for [garment workers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textile_industry_in_Bangladesh "Textile industry in Bangladesh") from [৳](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladeshi_taka "Bangladeshi taka") 12,500 to ৳ 17,000 following a 4–2 vote, a decision rejected by [labour unions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trade_union "Trade union") demanding at least ৳ 18,000 amid nearly a month of [labour unrest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strike_action "Strike action") and hundreds of factory closures. [(The Daily Star)](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/bangladesh/news/new-rmg-pay-scale-minimum-wage-set-tk-17k-after-vote-4332751)
    * At least 87 [garment factories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textile_industry_in_Bangladesh "Textile industry in Bangladesh") close across [Gazipur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gazipur_District "Gazipur District"), [Savar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savar_Upazila "Savar Upazila"), and [Ashulia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashulia "Ashulia"), after workers reject the newly announced [minimum wage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_wage "Minimum wage") as insufficient, blocking the [Dhaka–Mymensingh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N3_%28Bangladesh%29 "N3 (Bangladesh)") and [Dhaka–Tangail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N4_%28Bangladesh%29 "N4 (Bangladesh)") highways and clashing with [police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Police "Bangladesh Police") firing [tear gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tear_gas "Tear gas") and [stun grenades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stun_grenade "Stun grenade"). [(*The Business Standard*)](https://www.tbsnews.net/economy/rmg/police-fire-tear-gas-sound-grenades-garment-workers-block-dhaka-tangail-highway-1613231) [(*The Daily Star*)](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/news/87-garment-factories-shut-amid-protests-gazipur-savar-ashulia-4332861)
