#### Business and economy

* [2026 Bangladesh garment workers protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Bangladesh_garment_workers_protests "2026 Bangladesh garment workers protests")
  * At least 80 [garment factories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textile_industry_in_Bangladesh "Textile industry in Bangladesh") in [Savar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savar_Upazila "Savar Upazila") and [Ashulia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashulia "Ashulia"), [Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh"), declare general holidays after workers stage [sit-ins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sit-in "Sit-in") and [work stoppages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strike_action "Strike action") over [minimum wage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_wage "Minimum wage") demands, while [police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Police "Bangladesh Police") announce that 96 people, including five [labour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trade_union "Trade union") leaders, have been arrested in connection with the 4 November clashes on the [Dhaka–Aricha Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N5_%28Bangladesh%29 "N5 (Bangladesh)"). [(*The Business Standard*](https://www.tbsnews.net/economy/rmg/labour-unrest-least-12-factories-declare-holiday-amid-work-abstention-savar-1609261) [(*The Daily Star*)](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/news/50-factories-savar-ashulia-declare-holidays-amid-wage-protests-4329371)
  * Laid-off garment workers in [Rupganj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rupganj_Upazila "Rupganj Upazila"), [Narayanganj District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narayanganj_District "Narayanganj District"), block roads by [felling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felling "Felling") trees and [burning tires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tire_fire "Tire fire") over [unpaid wages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wage_theft "Wage theft"). [(*The Business Standard*)](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/garment-workers-block-road-over-unpaid-wages-narayanganj-1609671)
  * [Border Guard Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_Guard_Bangladesh "Border Guard Bangladesh") deploys 90 [platoons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platoon "Platoon") across seven [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Bangladesh "Districts of Bangladesh"), with four more on standby, amid ongoing [political violence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_violence "Political violence") and [strike action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strike_action "Strike action"). [(*Dhaka Tribune*)](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/425296/90-bgb-platoons-deployed-in-seven-districts)
