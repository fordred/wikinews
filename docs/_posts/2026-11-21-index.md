#### Business and economy

* [2026 Bangladesh garment workers protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Bangladesh_garment_workers_protests "2026 Bangladesh garment workers protests")
  * At least 50 [garment factories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textile_industry_in_Bangladesh "Textile industry in Bangladesh") in [Savar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savar_Upazila "Savar Upazila") and [Ashulia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashulia "Ashulia"), [Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh"), declare general holidays after workers stage [sit-ins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sit-in "Sit-in") and [work stoppages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strike_action "Strike action") over [minimum wage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_wage "Minimum wage") demands, while [police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Police "Bangladesh Police") announce that 96 people, including five [labour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trade_union "Trade union") leaders, have been arrested in connection with the 4 November clashes on the [Dhaka–Aricha Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N5_%28Bangladesh%29 "N5 (Bangladesh)").
