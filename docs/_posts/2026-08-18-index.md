* [Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore "Singapore") begins requiring major [Internet messaging platforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_messaging_platform "Internet messaging platform") to restrict unsolicited [contacts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_networking_service "Social networking service") and [social media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_media "Social media") services to verify [advertisers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_advertising "Online advertising") and block suspected [scam advertisements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_fraud "Internet fraud") under new anti-scam rules, with penalties of up to [S$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_dollar "Singapore dollar")1 million ([US$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_dollar "United States dollar")780,000) for non-compliance. [(AFP via *The Manila Times*)](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/08/18/world/singapore-tightens-anti-scam-rules-on-social-media-messaging-platforms/2407426)
