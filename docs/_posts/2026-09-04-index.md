#### Health and environment

* [New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand "New Zealand") becomes the second country after [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia "Australia") to authorize [pharmaceutical-grade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmaceutical-grade "Pharmaceutical-grade") [MDMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MDMA "MDMA") for severe [post-traumatic stress disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-traumatic_stress_disorder "Post-traumatic stress disorder"), with [Medsafe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medsafe "Medsafe") allowing two [psychiatrists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_health_in_New_Zealand "Mental health in New Zealand") to prescribe it alongside [psychotherapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MDMA-assisted_psychotherapy "MDMA-assisted psychotherapy") in controlled clinical settings. [(AFP via France 24)](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20260904-new-zealand-approves-use-of-party-drug-mdma-to-treat-trauma)
