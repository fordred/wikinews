#### Health and environment

* [New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand "New Zealand") becomes the second country after [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia "Australia") to authorize [pharmaceutical-grade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmaceutical-grade "Pharmaceutical-grade") [MDMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MDMA "MDMA") for severe [post-traumatic stress disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-traumatic_stress_disorder "Post-traumatic stress disorder"), with [Medsafe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medsafe "Medsafe") allowing two [psychiatrists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_health_in_New_Zealand "Mental health in New Zealand") to prescribe it alongside [psychotherapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MDMA-assisted_psychotherapy "MDMA-assisted psychotherapy") in controlled clinical settings. [(AFP via France 24)](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20260904-new-zealand-approves-use-of-party-drug-mdma-to-treat-trauma)

#### Law and crime

* A [Philippine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippines "Philippines") [regional trial court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_trial_court "Regional trial court") issues an arrest warrant for [vice president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_the_Philippines "Vice President of the Philippines") [Sara Duterte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Duterte "Sara Duterte") on three counts of [grave threats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_threat "Death threat") against [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Philippines "President of the Philippines") [Bongbong Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bongbong_Marcos "Bongbong Marcos"), [first lady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Lady_of_the_Philippines "First Lady of the Philippines") [Liza Araneta Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liza_Araneta_Marcos "Liza Araneta Marcos"), and former [House speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_House_of_Representatives_of_the_Philippines "Speaker of the House of Representatives of the Philippines") [Martin Romualdez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Romualdez "Martin Romualdez"), setting bail at [₱](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_peso "Philippine peso")120,000 ([US$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US%24 "US$")2,000) per count. [(Reuters)](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/philippine-court-issues-warrant-vp-dutertes-arrest-in-grave-threats-case-2026-09-04/)
* [Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People%27s_Police_%28China%29 "People's Police (China)") in [Guangxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guangxi "Guangxi"), [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China "China"), arrest 52 [Singaporeans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singaporeans "Singaporeans") over suspected involvement in illegal [pyramid-scheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyramid_scheme "Pyramid scheme") activities as part of a broader [law-enforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement_in_China "Law enforcement in China") operation.
