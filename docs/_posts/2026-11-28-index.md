#### Politics and elections

* [2026 Victorian state election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Victorian_state_election "2026 Victorian state election")
  * [Victorians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_%28state%29 "Victoria (state)") vote to elect 88 seats of the [Legislative Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victorian_Legislative_Assembly "Victorian Legislative Assembly") and [40 seats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Victorian_Legislative_Council_election "2026 Victorian Legislative Council election") of the [Legislative Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victorian_Legislative_Council "Victorian Legislative Council"). Incumbent [premier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_of_Victoria "Premier of Victoria") [Ben Carroll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Carroll "Ben Carroll") seeks to win a fourth consecutive [Labor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victorian_Labor_Party "Victorian Labor Party") government over the [Liberal–National Coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal%E2%80%93National_Coalition_%28Victoria%29 "Liberal–National Coalition (Victoria)"), led by [opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leader_of_the_Opposition_%28Victoria%29 "Leader of the Opposition (Victoria)") leader [Jess Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jess_Wilson "Jess Wilson").
