#### Disasters and accidents

* [2026 Nepal floods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Nepal_floods "2026 Nepal floods")
  * Major casualties are reported and many are reported missing in the [Tibet Autonomous Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tibet_Autonomous_Region "Tibet Autonomous Region") after a [mudslide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mudflow "Mudflow") strikes between [Tibet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tibet "Tibet") and [Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal "Nepal"), severely affecting [Gyirong County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyirong_County "Gyirong County").
