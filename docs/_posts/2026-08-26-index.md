#### Disasters and accidents

* "Major" casualties are reported and many are reported missing in [Tibet Autonomous Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tibet_Autonomous_Region "Tibet Autonomous Region") after a [mudslide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mudflow "Mudflow") strikes between [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China "China") and [Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal "Nepal"), severely affecting [Gyirong County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyirong_County "Gyirong County"). At least eight deaths are also reported in Nepal. [(Al Arabiya English)](https://english.alarabiya.net/News/world/2026/08/26/nepal-floods-kill-at-least-8-mudslide-causes-major-casualties-in-tibet) [(*The Straits Times*)](https://www.straitstimes.com/asia/tibets-gyirong-suffers-major-casualties-from-mudslide-xinhua-reports)
* At least 15 infants are killed and another is injured after a fire at a state-run hospital in [Islamabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamabad "Islamabad"), [Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan "Pakistan"). [(*The Globe and Mail*)](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/world/article-at-least-15-infants-killed-in-pakistan-hospital-fire-local-media/)
