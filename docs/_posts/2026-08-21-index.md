#### Armed conflicts and attacks

* [Insurgency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insurgency_in_Khyber_Pakhtunkhwa "Insurgency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa")
  * [Pakistani forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Armed_Forces "Pakistan Armed Forces") raid multiple hideouts and kill 49 militants allegedly belonging to [Fitna al Khawarij](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fitna_al_Khawarij "Fitna al Khawarij") and [Fitna Al Hindustan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fitna_Al_Hindustan "Fitna Al Hindustan") in [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khyber_Pakhtunkhwa "Khyber Pakhtunkhwa"), [Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan "Pakistan"). [(AP)](https://apnews.com/article/pakistan-security-forces-widen-operation-against-militants-e5dadb257138c1b59b3d821d23fe2fff)
* [Myanmar civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanmar_civil_war_%282021%E2%80%93present%29 "Myanmar civil war (2021–present)")
  * [Resistance fighters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ethnic_armed_organisations_in_Myanmar "List of ethnic armed organisations in Myanmar") and a local activist say [Myanmar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanmar "Myanmar")’s [military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatmadaw "Tatmadaw") deployed hundreds of troops around the proposed [Dawei Special Economic Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dawei_Special_Economic_Zone "Dawei Special Economic Zone") in the [Tanintharyi Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanintharyi_Region "Tanintharyi Region"), burning villages and pushing resistance groups back as [the government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Myanmar "Government of Myanmar") seeks to revive the project. [(Reuters)](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/myanmar-military-offensive-targets-land-russia-backed-port-project-2026-08-21/)

#### Health and environment

* [Project Elephant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Elephant "Project Elephant")
  * [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India "India")'s [Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_India "Supreme Court of India") orders [states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_and_union_territories_of_India "States and union territories of India") to protect [elephant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_elephant "Indian elephant") corridors, identify and remove obstructions, and prohibit groups from using fire to drive wild elephants away amid increasing [habitat loss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habitat_loss "Habitat loss") and [human-elephant conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human-elephant_conflict "Human-elephant conflict"). [(AFP via France 24)](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20260821-top-india-court-orders-new-steps-to-protect-threatened-elephants)

#### Law and crime

* The [Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong "Hong Kong") [Court of First Instance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_Court_of_First_Instance "Hong Kong Court of First Instance") convicts activists [Lee Cheuk-yan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Cheuk-yan "Lee Cheuk-yan") and [Chow Hang-tung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chow_Hang-tung "Chow Hang-tung") of [incitement to subversion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inciting_subversion_of_state_power "Inciting subversion of state power") over their leadership of [a group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_Alliance_in_Support_of_Patriotic_Democratic_Movements_of_China "Hong Kong Alliance in Support of Patriotic Democratic Movements of China") that organized annual [Tiananmen crackdown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Tiananmen_Square_protests_and_massacre "1989 Tiananmen Square protests and massacre") vigils. They face up to 10 years in [prison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prisons_in_Hong_Kong "Prisons in Hong Kong").
