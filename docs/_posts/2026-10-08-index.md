#### Arts and culture

* [2026 Nobel Prize in Literature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Nobel_Prize_in_Literature "2026 Nobel Prize in Literature")
  * Canadian poet [Anne Carson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne_Carson "Anne Carson") is awarded the [Nobel Prize in Literature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Literature "Nobel Prize in Literature") for "her bold and inventive oeuvre that, in playful dialogue with the classical tradition, has created new forms ‌for contemporary literature". [(BBC)](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cq4g1j54nepyo)
* The [Bank of England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_of_England "Bank of England") announces that the [Atlantic puffin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_puffin "Atlantic puffin"), [barn owl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barn_owl "Barn owl"), [buff-tailed bumblebee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombus_terrestris "Bombus terrestris") and [European hedgehog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_hedgehog "European hedgehog") will feature on the next set of [pound sterling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pound_sterling "Pound sterling") notes to be issued in the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom "United Kingdom"). It will be the first time animals have replaced people on banknotes in [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England "England"), which is intended to increase awareness of [British wildlife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fauna_of_Great_Britain "Fauna of Great Britain"). [(BBC News)](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwe8ld517ry3o)
