#### Law and crime

* [2026 French lycée protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_French_lyc%C3%A9e_protests "2026 French lycée protests")
  * Mass [rioting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riot "Riot") continues for a third day across [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France "France") with hundreds reportedly injured and over 500 [schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_in_France "Education in France") shut, while more than 2,000 people are arrested. [(BBC News)](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/live/cmx2z20vz124t)
  * [French government ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_France "Government of France"), including right-wing [education minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_education_ministers_of_France "List of education ministers of France") [Édouard Geffray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89douard_Geffray "Édouard Geffray"), accuse the [far-left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far-left_politics_in_France "Far-left politics in France") of inciting the youth-led unrest across the country. [Intelligence agencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_intelligence_agencies_of_France "List of intelligence agencies of France") briefing the government during a crisis meeting on the riots accuse the left-wing [La France Insoumise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_France_Insoumise "La France Insoumise") of actively inciting people to join the protests. [(AFP via NDTV)](https://www.ndtv.com/world-news/france-blames-radical-left-for-violence-amid-intensifying-school-protests-12127772) [(Ynet News)](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/sjrfd995fl)
