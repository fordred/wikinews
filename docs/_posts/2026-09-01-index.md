  * The [International Atomic Energy Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Atomic_Energy_Agency "International Atomic Energy Agency") reports that [Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syria "Syria")'s [former government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_of_Bashar_al-Assad "Presidency of Bashar al-Assad") under [Bashar al-Assad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bashar_al-Assad "Bashar al-Assad") failed to declare [nuclear material](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_material "Nuclear material") and [facilities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_power_plant "Nuclear power plant"), confirming that infrastructure at [Deir ez-Zor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deir_ez-Zor "Deir ez-Zor") is consistent with an unfinished [nuclear reactor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_reactor "Nuclear reactor") and identifying about 73 tonnes of [natural uranium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_uranium "Natural uranium"). [(AFP via France 24)](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20260901-syria-site-infrastructure-consistent-with-nuclear-reactor-iaea)
* The [Turkish foreign ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_%28Turkey%29 "Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Turkey)") reports that [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey "Turkey") has joined the U.S.-led [Artemis Accords](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artemis_Accords "Artemis Accords"), becoming the 71st country to do so. [(AA)](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/science-technology/turkiye-joins-nasa-s-artemis-accords-as-71st-signatory-to-peaceful-space-exploration-pact/4043176) [(*Firstpost*)](https://www.firstpost.com/world/turkey-becomes-71st-country-to-join-us-led-artemis-accords-for-space-exploration-14042345.html)

#### Sports

* [2026–27 NBA season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026%E2%80%9327_NBA_season "2026–27 NBA season")
  * In [basketball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball "Basketball"), the [NBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Basketball_Association "National Basketball Association") announces that the [Los Angeles Clippers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Clippers "Los Angeles Clippers") had [violated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Clippers_salary_cap_scandal "Los Angeles Clippers salary cap scandal") the [league salary cap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_salary_cap "NBA salary cap") by signing [Kawhi Leonard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawhi_Leonard "Kawhi Leonard") in 2022 via a series of external dealings. Penalties include fines of [US$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US%24 "US$")30 million for the Clippers and $700,000 for Leonard, the loss of the Clippers' [first-round picks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Draft_%28sports%29 "Draft (sports)") in each [draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_draft "NBA draft") from 2029 to 2033, and a one-year suspension for Clippers owner [Steve Ballmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Ballmer "Steve Ballmer"). [(AFP via *Daily Tribune*)](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/09/03/nba-cracks-whip-on-clippers-over-salary-cap-row) [(ESPN)](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/49806356/nba-announces-punishments-clippers-kawhi-probe)
* In [baseball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baseball "Baseball"), the [Los Angeles Angels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Angels "Los Angeles Angels") of the [MLB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_League_Baseball "Major League Baseball") are sold to [Stan Kroenke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stan_Kroenke "Stan Kroenke") of [Kroenke Sports & Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kroenke_Sports_%26_Entertainment "Kroenke Sports & Entertainment") for an unspecified price, ending 23 years of ownership by [Arte Moreno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arte_Moreno "Arte Moreno"). The deal faces pending regulatory review and is expected to be complete by 2027. [(CNBC)](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/stan-kroenke-controlling-stake-los-angeles-angels.html) [(Reuters)](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/kroenke-reaches-deal-add-angels-sports-portfolio-2026-09-01/)
