#### Politics and elections

* [2026 United States elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_elections "2026 United States elections")
  * The [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States "United States") holds [midterm elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_midterm_election "United States midterm election") for 435 [members](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections "2026 United States House of Representatives elections") and five [non-voting delegates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-voting_members_of_the_United_States_House_of_Representatives "Non-voting members of the United States House of Representatives") of the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_House_of_Representatives "United States House of Representatives"), 35 [members](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_elections "2026 United States Senate elections") of the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Senate "United States Senate") (including special elections in [Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_special_election_in_Florida "2026 United States Senate special election in Florida") and [Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_special_election_in_Ohio "2026 United States Senate special election in Ohio")), 39 state and territorial [governors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_gubernatorial_elections "2026 United States gubernatorial elections"), and numerous other [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_state_legislative_elections "2026 United States state legislative elections"), [territorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_state_legislative_elections#Territorial_and_federal_district_summaries "2026 United States state legislative elections"), [tribal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Navajo_Nation_presidential_election "2026 Navajo Nation presidential election"), and [local](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_local_elections "2026 United States local elections") officials.
