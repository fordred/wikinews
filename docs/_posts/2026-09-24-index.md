#### Law and crime

* [Chromebook procurement scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromebook_procurement_scandal "Chromebook procurement scandal")
  * A [court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary_of_Indonesia "Judiciary of Indonesia") in [Jakarta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakarta "Jakarta"), [Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesia "Indonesia"), reduces former [education minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_and_Culture_%28Indonesia%29 "Ministry of Education and Culture (Indonesia)") and [Gojek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gojek "Gojek") co-founder [Nadiem Makarim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nadiem_Makarim "Nadiem Makarim")'s prison sentence from 10 to nine years for [corruption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corruption_in_Indonesia "Corruption in Indonesia") involving [Chromebook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromebook "Chromebook") procurement, while lowering [restitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Restitution_and_unjust_enrichment "Restitution and unjust enrichment") from [Rp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_rupiah "Indonesian rupiah")809 billion ([US$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US%24 "US$")45 million) to Rp548 billion ($30 million).
