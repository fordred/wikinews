#### Sports

* In [association football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football "Association football"), [Major League Soccer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_League_Soccer "Major League Soccer") announces that all 27 teams from the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States "United States") will return to the [U.S. Open Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Open_Cup "U.S. Open Cup") beginning in 2027, making it the first full participation in the tournament since [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_U.S._Open_Cup "2023 U.S. Open Cup"). [(ESPN)](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/49795119/us-mls-teams-make-full-return-open-cup-2027)
* In [basketball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball "Basketball"), the [NBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Basketball_Association "National Basketball Association") announces the results of its investigation into the [Los Angeles Clippers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Clippers "Los Angeles Clippers")' signing of star player [Kawhi Leonard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawhi_Leonard "Kawhi Leonard") in 2022. The league determines that the Clippers violated the [league salary cap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_salary_cap "NBA salary cap") via a series of external dealings. Penalties include fines of $30 million for the Clippers and $700,000 for Leonard, the loss of the Clippers' first-round picks in each [draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_draft "NBA draft") from 2029–2033, and a one-year suspension for Clippers owner [Steve Ballmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Ballmer "Steve Ballmer"). [(ESPN)](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/49806356/nba-announces-punishments-clippers-kawhi-probe)
