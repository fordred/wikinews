* [Yemeni civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemeni_civil_war_%282014%E2%80%93present%29 "Yemeni civil war (2014–present)")
  * The [Houthis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houthis "Houthis") launch an offensive in western [Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemen "Yemen"), clashing with the [Yemeni Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemeni_Armed_Forces "Yemeni Armed Forces") and aiming to reach and cut the road linking [Taiz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiz "Taiz") to [Al-Makha district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Makha_district "Al-Makha district"). Fourteen [balistic missiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balistic_missile "Balistic missile") and several [drones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drone_warfare "Drone warfare") are launched in Taiz and [Hodeidah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hodeidah "Hodeidah"). At least 18 Houthi fighters and 13 government troops are killed in the clashes.
