#### Politics and elections

* [2026 Bosnian general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Bosnian_general_election "2026 Bosnian general election")
  * [Bosnians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosnia_and_Herzegovina "Bosnia and Herzegovina") vote to elect the three members of the [presidency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina "Presidency of Bosnia and Herzegovina") and 42 members of the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina "House of Representatives of Bosnia and Herzegovina"). Concurrent elections are also held in the [Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina "Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina") to [elect 98 seats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Federation_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina_general_election "2026 Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina general election") of the [regional legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_of_the_Federation_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina "House of Representatives of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina") and in [Republika Srpska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republika_Srpska "Republika Srpska") to [elect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Republika_Srpska_general_election "2026 Republika Srpska general election") the entity's [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Republika_Srpska "President of Republika Srpska") and the 83 seats of the [respective legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_%28Republika_Srpska%29 "National Assembly (Republika Srpska)").
