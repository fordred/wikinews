#### Politics and elections

* [2026 Bosnian general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Bosnian_general_election "2026 Bosnian general election")
  * [Bosnians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosnia_and_Herzegovina "Bosnia and Herzegovina") vote to elect the three members of the [presidency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina "Presidency of Bosnia and Herzegovina") and 42 members of the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina "House of Representatives of Bosnia and Herzegovina"). Concurrent elections are also held in the [Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina "Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina") to [elect 98 seats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Federation_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina_general_election "2026 Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina general election") of the [regional legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_of_the_Federation_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina "House of Representatives of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina") and in [Republika Srpska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republika_Srpska "Republika Srpska") to [elect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Republika_Srpska_general_election "2026 Republika Srpska general election") the entity's [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Republika_Srpska "President of Republika Srpska") and the 83 seats of the [respective legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_%28Republika_Srpska%29 "National Assembly (Republika Srpska)"). [(AFP)](https://www.afp.com/en/bosnia-votes-wartime-divides-drive-campaigns)
* [2026 Brazilian general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Brazilian_general_election "2026 Brazilian general election")
  * [Brazilians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilians "Brazilians") head to the polls to elect their [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Brazil "President of Brazil"), [vice president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_Brazil "Vice President of Brazil"), 513 members of [Chamber of Deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_of_Deputies "Chamber of Deputies") and 54 members in the [Federal Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Senate_%28Brazil%29 "Federal Senate (Brazil)"). Incumbent president [Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luiz_In%C3%A1cio_Lula_da_Silva "Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva") is running for the fourth non-consecutive term, where his main opponent will be [Flávio Bolsonaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fl%C3%A1vio_Bolsonaro "Flávio Bolsonaro"), [Senator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Senate "Brazilian Senate") for [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro_%28state%29 "Rio de Janeiro (state)") and son of former president [Jair Bolsonaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jair_Bolsonaro "Jair Bolsonaro"). [(The Guardian)](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/04/brazil-presidential-election-lula-flavio-bolsonaro)
