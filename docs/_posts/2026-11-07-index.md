#### Politics and elections

* [2026 New Zealand general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_New_Zealand_general_election "2026 New Zealand general election")
  * [New Zealanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealanders "New Zealanders") vote to elect 120 members of the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_House_of_Representatives "New Zealand House of Representatives"), with 11 additional [overhang seats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overhang_seat "Overhang seat"). [National](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_National_Party "New Zealand National Party") leader [Christopher Luxon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Luxon "Christopher Luxon") and [Labour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Labour_Party "New Zealand Labour Party") leader [Chris Hipkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hipkins "Chris Hipkins") both seek reappointment as the country's [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_New_Zealand "Prime Minister of New Zealand"). Preliminary results see Labour lead in the [popular vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_vote "Popular vote") and the Nationals having the most amount of seats, with [special votes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_vote "Special vote") yet to be counted.
