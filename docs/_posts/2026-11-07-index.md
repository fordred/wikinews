#### Politics and elections

* [2026 New Zealand general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_New_Zealand_general_election "2026 New Zealand general election")
  * [New Zealanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealanders "New Zealanders") vote to elect 120 members of the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_House_of_Representatives "New Zealand House of Representatives"), with additional [overhang seats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overhang_seat "Overhang seat").
