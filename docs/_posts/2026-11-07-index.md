  * [Kordofan campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kordofan_campaign_%282023%E2%80%93present%29 "Kordofan campaign (2023–present)")

  * Six people are killed and 15 more injured in a [drive-by](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drive-by_shooting "Drive-by shooting") [mass shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_shooting "Mass shooting") in the center of [Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krak%C3%B3w "Kraków"), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland "Poland"). The perpetrator, who also killed his parents and his sister at their home in [Ostrowiec Świętokrzyski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ostrowiec_%C5%9Awi%C4%99tokrzyski "Ostrowiec Świętokrzyski"), is wounded by [police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_of_Poland "Police of Poland") and arrested. [(Reuters)](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/two-kiled-least-10-injured-shooting-polish-city-krakow-tvn24-reports-2026-11-07/)

  * [Saudi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Arabia "Saudi Arabia") [airstrikes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airstrike "Airstrike") hit homes near [Sanaa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanaa "Sanaa"), [Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemen "Yemen"), killing at least 27 people and wounding 36 others.   * [New Zealanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealanders "New Zealanders") vote to elect 120 members of the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_House_of_Representatives "New Zealand House of Representatives"), with 11 additional [overhang seats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overhang_seat "Overhang seat"). Preliminary results see Labour lead in the [popular vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_vote "Popular vote") and the Nationals having the most amount of seats, with [special votes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_vote "Special vote") yet to be counted.
