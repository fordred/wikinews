* [2026 Budhi Gandaki flood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Budhi_Gandaki_flood "2026 Budhi Gandaki flood")

The exact number of casualties is unknown. #### Health and environment

* [2026 Indonesia wildfires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Indonesia_wildfires "2026 Indonesia wildfires")
